# Code of Conduct

Noxis aims to be a rigorous, welcoming technical community. Participate in
good faith, distinguish evidence from speculation, and treat contributors with
respect.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal
attacks, doxxing, threats, and publication of vulnerability details outside
the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

If a conduct concern arises, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub.
Reports will be handled discreetly and proportionately. Project participation
may be limited when behavior undermines a safe, constructive collaboration.
